FLEET OPERATIONS / PAYROLL

Driver payroll

Each route has two payroll rows: one for the lead driver and one for the co-driver. Set independent rates and extras for each.
Prototype payroll estimates only. Route pay rates, driver assignments and mileage are entered here and saved in this browser. Payroll is not submitted or synchronized with the legacy payroll system. Hourly pay uses entered hours, per diem uses entered eligible days, and special pay is entered per route. Review overtime and per diem tax treatment separately. Lead and co-driver pay are calculated separately. Route revenue-based pay is an estimate and may count a shipment on more than one route; confirm your policy before processing.

OPERATIONS INTELLIGENCE

Reports & mileage

Export demo shipment data; connect historical mileage and reports from the current system during integration.

Route profitability — revenue minus expenses

Revenue is attributed to the delivery route only (to avoid double-counting pickup and delivery). Includes the $150 minimum charge on non-prepaid orders. Expenses are recorded route expenses; unrecorded costs are not included.

Date filtering uses order delivery dates and expense dates. Expenses without a date appear only when no date filter is set.
